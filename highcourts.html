#AllahabadHC#Section74#ITC#SamsungDisplay
Hon'ble Allahabad High Court has granted interim relief by staying proceedings under a Section 74 SCN holding that the Revenue cannot invoke the extended period of limitation merely because the taxpayer availed ITC on Plant and Machinery without declaring Restricted ITC (RITC).
